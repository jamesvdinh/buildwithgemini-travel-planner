## 🗓️ Day-by-Day Schedule

### **Day 1: Historic Asakusa & Traditional Culture (Tokyo)**
* **Morning:** Visit **Sensō-ji Temple** (4.7 ⭐ | Free Admission | Grounds open 24/7, Main Hall 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple) and walk along Nakamise Shopping Street. Stop by the **Asakusa Culture Tourist Info Center (8th Floor Deck)** for a panorama view of Sensō-ji and Tokyo Skytree.
  * *Tip: Arrive before 8:30 AM to enjoy the grounds before peak crowds.*
  ![Sensō-ji Temple](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1545569341-9eb8b30979d9?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)
* **Mid-Day:** Lunch at historic tempura spots like **Daikokuya** or **Sansada** (traditional sesame oil *Tendon*), then explore **Kappabashi Kitchen Street** (famous for chef knives and wax food models).
* **Afternoon:** Stroll along the **Sumida Riverwalk**, then explore Ueno Park and the **Tokyo National Museum**.
* **Evening:** Local dining at Hoppy Dori in Asakusa for yakitori, stew, and drinks.

### **Day 2: Tsukiji Outer Market & Imperial Gardens (Tokyo)**
* **Morning:** Street food tasting tour at **Tsukiji Outer Market** (sushi, tamagoyaki, fresh seafood).
* **Afternoon:** Stroll around Imperial Palace East Gardens and Ginza. Tea break at Café de L'Ambre or Chazen Tea House.
* **Evening:** High-end Omakase sushi dinner at **Ginza Kyubey** (legendary Ginza sushi establishment).
  ![Specialty Dining](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528164344705-47542687990d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)
  *🚨 Reservation Note: Must book 1–2 months in advance via hotel concierge / Tableall.*

### **Day 3: Shibuya Crossing & Modern Vibes (Tokyo)**
* **Morning:** Walk through **Meiji Jingu Shrine** & Harajuku.
* **Afternoon:** Cross the famous **Shibuya Scramble** and view Tokyo from **Shibuya Sky**.
  ![Tokyo City View](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1542051841857-5f90071e7989?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)
  *🚨 Reservation Note: Shibuya Sky sunset slots require booking 4 weeks in advance.*
* **Evening:** Traditional Izakaya dinner & night walk in Shinjuku Memory Lane (Omoide Yokocho) & Kabukicho.
  ![Noodle & Izakaya Spot](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1569718212165-3a8278d5f624?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

### **Day 4: Shinkansen to Kyoto & Fushimi Inari Sunset**
* **Morning:** Take the Shinkansen Bullet Train from Tokyo to Kyoto Station. Check into Kyoto accommodation.
* **Afternoon:** Explore **Fushimi Inari Taisha** and hike through the iconic Senbon Torii gates.
  ![Kyoto Cultural Landmark](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1493976040374-85c8e12f0c0e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)
* **Evening:** Dinner at Fushimi Inari (enjoying local specialty dining such as Kendonya Kitsune Udon or local noodle spots nearby).

### **Day 5: Historic Kyoto & Gion District**
* **Morning:** Visit **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** and stroll through the historic Sannen-zaka & Ninen-zaka preserves.
* **Afternoon:** Tour Gion District, Yasaka Shrine, and experience a Traditional Tea Ceremony at Camellia Tea House.
  *🚨 Reservation Note: Reserve tea ceremony 1–2 weeks ahead.*
* **Evening:** Farewell Kaiseki dinner along Pontocho Alley.
  ![Specialty Dining](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528164344705-47542687990d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)
  *🚨 Reservation Note: Book Pontocho riverside Kaiseki dining 1–2 months ahead.*
